PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi Scheme
Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)

This document gives a short overview of PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi Scheme.

PM-KISAN was launched in February 2019 to give income support to farmer families. Eligible farmer families receive 6,000 rupees per year under PM-KISAN. The amount is paid in three equal instalments of 2,000 rupees every four months.

The money is transferred directly into the bank accounts of beneficiaries through Direct Benefit Transfer. PM-KISAN is a Central Sector scheme, fully funded by the Government of India. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

At the start the scheme covered only small and marginal farmers with up to 2 hectares of land, but it was extended to all landholding farmer families in June 2019. Beneficiaries must complete e-KYC and link Aadhaar with their bank account to keep receiving instalments.
