# MGNREGA Rural Employment Guarantee

*Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)*

This document gives a short overview of MGNREGA Rural Employment Guarantee.

## Key points

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act was enacted in 2005 and was originally called NREGA. It guarantees up to 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer for unskilled manual work. An interested household must apply for a Job Card at the local Gram Panchayat.

Work must be provided within 15 days of a demand, failing which an unemployment allowance is payable. At least one third of the beneficiaries must be women. Wages are paid directly into workers' bank or post office accounts.

Typical works include water conservation, drought proofing, rural roads and land development. The programme is demand driven, and social audits by the Gram Sabha are mandatory for transparency.
