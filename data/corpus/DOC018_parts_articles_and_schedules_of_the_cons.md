# Parts, Articles and Schedules of the Constitution

*Source: Constitution of India (public document, legislative.gov.in / India Code)*

This document gives a short overview of Parts, Articles and Schedules of the Constitution.

## Key points

The original Constitution of 1950 had 395 articles, 22 parts and 8 schedules. The Constitution now contains 12 schedules after later amendments. The First Schedule lists the states and union territories of India.

The Seventh Schedule contains the Union, State and Concurrent Lists. The Eighth Schedule lists the officially recognised languages of India, which currently number 22. The Tenth Schedule, added in 1985, contains the anti-defection provisions.

The Eleventh Schedule lists 29 subjects for Panchayats and the Twelfth Schedule lists 18 subjects for municipalities. The Constitution of India is one of the longest written constitutions in the world.
