Stand-Up India Scheme

Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)

This document gives a short overview of Stand-Up India Scheme.

Stand-Up India was launched on 5 April 2016 to promote entrepreneurship among women and marginalised communities. The scheme facilitates bank loans between 10 lakh and 1 crore rupees. Each bank branch is expected to lend to at least one Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe borrower and at least one woman borrower.

The loan is meant for setting up a greenfield enterprise in manufacturing, services or the trading sector. A greenfield enterprise means the first-time venture of the beneficiary. The Small Industries Development Bank of India supports the scheme with refinance.

Borrowers can use a Stand-Up India portal to apply and get handholding support. The repayment period can be up to seven years with a moratorium of up to 18 months.
