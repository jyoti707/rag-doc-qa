Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)

This document gives a short overview of Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana.

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana was launched on 1 May 2016 from Ballia in Uttar Pradesh. The scheme provides LPG connections to women from below poverty line households. The Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas implements the scheme.

The original target was 5 crore connections, which was later increased to 8 crore. The connection is issued in the name of the woman of the household. Ujjwala 2.0 was launched on 10 August 2021 with a facility for migrants to use a self-declaration as address proof.

The aim of the scheme is to replace unclean cooking fuels such as firewood and cow dung with clean LPG. Clean cooking fuel reduces indoor air pollution and respiratory illnesses among women and children.
