Preamble and Making of the Constitution
Source: Constitution of India (public document, legislative.gov.in / India Code)

This document gives a short overview of Preamble and Making of the Constitution.

The Constitution of India was adopted by the Constituent Assembly on 26 November 1949. The Constitution came into force on 26 January 1950, a date now celebrated as Republic Day. The Preamble describes India as a sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic.

The words socialist, secular and integrity were inserted into the Preamble by the 42nd Amendment in 1976. The Preamble promises justice, liberty, equality and fraternity to all citizens. The Preamble opens with the words We, the people of India, showing that the Constitution draws its authority from the people.

Dr B. R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee of the Constituent Assembly. Dr Rajendra Prasad served as the President of the Constituent Assembly, which first met in December 1946.
