Swachh Bharat Mission
Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)

This document gives a short overview of Swachh Bharat Mission.

Swachh Bharat Mission was launched on 2 October 2014, the birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi. The mission has two parts, Swachh Bharat Mission Gramin for rural areas and Swachh Bharat Mission Urban for cities. Its main goal was to make India open defecation free by 2 October 2019, the 150th birth anniversary of Mahatma Gandhi.

Rural households were given an incentive of 12,000 rupees to build an individual household toilet. The mission also promotes solid and liquid waste management and scientific processing of garbage. Phase II of SBM Gramin, from 2020-21 to 2024-25, focused on ODF Plus, which sustains open defecation free status and manages waste.

The Ministry of Jal Shakti handles the rural component, while the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs handles the urban component. Swachh Survekshan is an annual survey that ranks cities on cleanliness.
