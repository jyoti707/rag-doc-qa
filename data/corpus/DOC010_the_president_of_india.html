The President of India

Source: Constitution of India (public document, legislative.gov.in / India Code)

This document gives a short overview of The President of India.

The President is the head of State of India and the executive power of the Union is formally vested in the President. A person must be a citizen of India and at least 35 years old to be eligible for the office of President. The President is elected by an electoral college made up of the elected members of both Houses of Parliament and the elected members of state legislative assemblies.

The President serves a term of five years and may be re-elected. The President can be removed from office by impeachment under Article 61 for violation of the Constitution. The Vice-President acts as the ex officio Chairman of the Rajya Sabha.

Under Article 74 the President must act on the aid and advice of the Council of Ministers headed by the Prime Minister. Article 123 empowers the President to promulgate ordinances when Parliament is not in session.
