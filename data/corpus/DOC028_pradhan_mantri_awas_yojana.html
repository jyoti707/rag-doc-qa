Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana

Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)

This document gives a short overview of Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana.

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana aims to provide housing for all and has an urban part launched on 25 June 2015. PMAY Urban has four verticals: in-situ slum redevelopment, credit linked subsidy, affordable housing in partnership and beneficiary-led construction. PMAY Gramin was launched in November 2016 to provide pucca houses to rural homeless households.

Unit assistance under PMAY Gramin is 1.20 lakh rupees in plain areas and 1.30 lakh rupees in hilly and difficult areas. The minimum size of a PMAY Gramin house is 25 square metres, with a space for hygienic cooking. Ownership of houses is preferably in the name of the woman or jointly with the husband.

The Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme gives an interest subsidy on home loans to economically weaker, low and middle income groups. PMAY Gramin beneficiaries are also linked to MGNREGA for 90 to 95 days of unskilled labour during house construction.
