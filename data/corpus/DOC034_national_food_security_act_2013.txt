National Food Security Act 2013
Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)

This document gives a short overview of National Food Security Act 2013.

The National Food Security Act, 2013 gives legal rights to subsidised foodgrains to a large part of the population. The Act covers up to 75 percent of the rural population and up to 50 percent of the urban population. Each eligible person under the priority households category receives 5 kg of foodgrains per month.

The subsidised prices were fixed at 3 rupees per kg for rice, 2 rupees for wheat and 1 rupee for coarse grains. Antyodaya Anna Yojana households, the poorest of the poor, get 35 kg of foodgrains per household per month. The eldest woman of the household, aged 18 or above, is treated as the head of the household for issuing ration cards.

Pregnant and lactating women are entitled to a maternity benefit of at least 6,000 rupees in instalments. Children aged 6 months to 14 years are entitled to meals under the Integrated Child Development Services and mid-day meal schemes.
