# Skill India Mission and PMKVY

*Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)*

This document gives a short overview of Skill India Mission and PMKVY.

## Key points

Skill India was launched on 15 July 2015, World Youth Skills Day, to train youth in employable skills. The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship was created in November 2014. The National Skill Development Corporation is a public-private partnership that funds skill training providers.

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana is the flagship skill certification scheme of the mission. The original target of Skill India was to train 40 crore people by 2022. Recognition of Prior Learning assesses and certifies skills that workers already have but do not hold a certificate for.

Sector Skill Councils set the occupational standards for each industry sector. The National Skills Qualification Framework organises qualifications by levels of knowledge and skill.
