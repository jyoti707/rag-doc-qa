# Digital India Programme

*Source: Government of India scheme guidelines and Press Information Bureau releases (public information)*

This document gives a short overview of Digital India Programme.

## Key points

The Digital India programme was launched on 1 July 2015 to transform India into a digitally empowered society. It rests on three vision areas: digital infrastructure as a core utility, governance and services on demand, and digital empowerment of citizens. BharatNet aims to connect gram panchayats with high-speed optical fibre broadband.

DigiLocker lets citizens store and share official documents such as certificates in digital form. The Unified Payments Interface, developed by NPCI, allows instant bank-to-bank payments using a mobile phone. The UMANG app offers many government services through a single mobile platform.

The programme is coordinated by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology. Digital India is structured around nine pillars, including broadband highways, e-governance and e-Kranti.
